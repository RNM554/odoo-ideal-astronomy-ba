# Fit-GAP Analysis – Odoo 18 Community

| Req ID | Business Requirement | Odoo 18 Standard Capability | Evaluation | Configuration / Workaround Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **REQ-01** | Product variants (magnification) & brand filtering on Shop page. | Supports Product Attributes & Variants natively, but lacks a standalone "Brand" field in Community edition. | **Partial Fit** | Configured `Brand` and `Magnification` as Product Attributes with `Instantly` variant creation and enabled attribute filters on the eCommerce sidebar. |
| **REQ-02** | Dedicated partner recognition banner on Homepage. | Website Builder provides customizable building blocks (Carousel, Columns, Image Gallery). | **Fit** | Configured a custom multi-column logo block on the homepage featuring Celestron, Orion, ZWO, and HAS. |
| **REQ-03** | Single clean CTA in top navigation bar & checkout payment. | Default theme includes both a menu link and a header CTA button for Contact; supports Demo Payment Provider. | **Partial Fit** | Customized header layout via Website Builder to remove the duplicate Contact CTA; activated Demo Payment Acquirer for end-to-end checkout testing. |
| **REQ-04** | Real-time website customer support widget. | Standard Odoo Live Chat module integrates natively with Website and Discuss. | **Fit** | Installed Live Chat, configured channel rules for Vietnamese visitors, and assigned internal operators. |
| **REQ-05** | B2B pipeline tracking prior to quotation. | Standard CRM module supports Kanban stages and 1-click conversion to Quotation. | **Fit** | Installed CRM and mapped pipeline stages to match institutional sales workflow. |
| **REQ-06** | Separate B2B and B2C pricing tiers. | Supports multiple Pricelists with formula-based or fixed pricing rules. | **Fit** | Enabled Pricelists in Sales Settings and configured B2C Retail vs. B2B Partner pricelists. |
| **REQ-07** | Standard shipping rates, VAT (5%/10%), and auto-confirmation emails. | Supports Delivery Methods, Tax rules, and Automated Email Templates out-of-the-box. | **Fit** | Configured `Giao hàng tiêu chuẩn` carrier, set up 5% and 10% Sales VAT taxes, and enabled automatic confirmation emails. |
| **REQ-08** | Auto-generation of delivery orders from confirmed Sales Orders. | Native tight integration between Sales and Inventory modules. | **Fit** | Configured 1-step delivery route (`Deliver goods directly`) with automatic stock reservation upon SO confirmation. |
| **REQ-09** | Invoice generation and payment recording from SO. | Standard Invoicing module supports invoicing based on ordered or delivered quantities. | **Fit** | Set invoicing policy to "Ordered quantities" and configured bank/cash journals for payment registration. |
| **REQ-10** | Executive sales dashboard & staff role segregation. | Community edition provides basic pivot/graph views in Reporting and standard User Access Groups (lacks Enterprise Spreadsheet Dashboards). | **GAP** | Utilized built-in Sales & Inventory Pivot/Graph reporting views with saved favorites, and mapped Employee records to granular module access rights. |
