# Business Requirements – Ideal Astronomy Store

## 1. Storefront & Catalog Management
| Req ID | Requirement | Acceptance Criteria | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **REQ-01** | Display astronomy equipment catalog for B2C and B2B customers. | - Products include telescopes, mounts, and accessories.<br>- Pricing visibility depends on user login status (B2B vs B2C). | High |
| **REQ-02** | Brand and Partner Recognition. | - Must prominently display official partner logos: Celestron, Orion, ZWO Astronomy, and Hội Thiên văn Hà Nội (HAS). | High |

## 2. Sales & Checkout Process
| Req ID | Requirement | Acceptance Criteria | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **REQ-03** | Streamlined B2C checkout flow. | - Guest checkout enabled.<br>- Redundant "Contact Us" button must be removed from the top navigation bar to reduce friction. | High |
| **REQ-04** | B2B Quotation workflow. | - B2B customers can request a quote instead of direct checkout.<br>- Sales team can review and convert quotes to Sales Orders. | Medium |
